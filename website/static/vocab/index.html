EyeDataHub metadata terms

This page documents the small EyeDataHub vocabulary used in the release JSON-LD catalog. Standard concepts use DCAT, Dublin Core Terms, and Schema.org. These project terms preserve catalog fields that do not have a direct standard equivalent.

Namespace: https://khosravipooya.com/EyeDataHub/vocab/#
JSON-LD context: context.jsonld

Terms

sourceLicenseStatement
The source license or access statement recorded in the catalog.
normalizedSourceTermGroup
A curatorial source term group used for filtering, not a legal determination.
accessBackend
The direct, platform, or manual route recorded for a resource.
task
A nonexclusive research or evaluation task associated with a resource.
sourceReportedRecords
An item count reported by the source. Units vary across resources.
doiString
A DOI string found in a source URL or citation. It may identify a dataset or a related publication.
independentSourceCheckStatus
The recorded status of an independent row-level source check.
registrySearchesThrough
The project-level end date for catalog discovery searches.
sourceCheckScope
A statement describing what the recorded source validation status does and does not establish.
